Privacy Policy

Ride the Bus (Discord Activity) · Last updated: July 3, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information the "Ride the Bus" Discord Activity (the "Service") accesses and how it is used. The Service is an independent card game that runs inside Discord voice channels and is not affiliated with Discord Inc.

1. Information We Access

When you launch the Service, Discord asks you to authorize it using the identify and guilds OAuth scopes. Through this, the Service accesses:

DataWhy
Your Discord user IDTo identify you as a player and manage whose turn it is.
Your username / display nameTo show who is playing and spectating.
Your avatarTo display next to your name in the game.
The activity instance IDTo group everyone in the same voice channel into the same game.

2. How We Use It

This information is used only to run the live game session — showing the player list, tracking the active player and spectators, and syncing the game board between everyone in the channel.

3. Data Retention

The Service does not use a database and does not permanently store your information. All game data is held temporarily in the server's memory for the duration of the session and is discarded when the game ends, when you leave, or when the server restarts. We do not build profiles or keep a history of your activity.

4. Cookies and Tracking

The Service does not use advertising or analytics cookies and does not track you across websites. The Discord authorization token used to sign you in is kept only in your browser's memory for the current session and is not stored.

5. Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your information with third parties. Data is exchanged only between your Discord client, Discord's own servers, and the Service's server in order to run the game.

6. Third-Party Services

The Service runs on top of Discord and is delivered through Discord's infrastructure. Your use of Discord is governed by Discord's Privacy Policy.

7. Children's Privacy

The Service is not directed to children under 13, and you must meet Discord's minimum age requirements to use it.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date above reflects the most recent revision.

9. Contact
